Picture the end of a long day at the CTICC. Lanyard in your pocket, feet still recovering from the exhibition floor, inbox blinking away. Now picture swapping the hotel corridor for a green garden, and the hotel bar for a five-minute walk to the beach, with Table Mountain glowing across the bay in the evening light. That's the trade we offer our business guests, and quite a few of them don't go back.

## Cape Town's conference calendar is filling up

The Cape Town International Convention Centre (CTICC) is busy for most of the year, but the next few months are especially full.

![Road sign pointing to the CTICC and the V&A Waterfront in Cape Town](images/cticc-waterfront-road-sign.jpg)

### Still this year

- **FAIS Congress**, the 13th congress of the Federation of African Immunological Societies: 30 November to 3 December 2026
- **ASLM 2026**, the biennial conference of the African Society for Laboratory Medicine: 6 to 11 December 2026

### Early 2027

- **Investing in African Mining Indaba:** 8 to 11 February 2027 at the CTICC, one of the key dates on the global mining calendar, when the whole city seems to turn into one big networking event
- **Africa Energy Indaba:** 2 to 4 March 2027 at the CTICC, bringing together energy ministries, utilities and investors from across the continent

If your company is sending a team, or you're exhibiting, now is a sensible time to sort out where everyone will sleep. All conference dates near the lodge are also in our [events calendar](/en/events/).

## Why stay outside the city centre?

Fair question. The CBD gives you the shortest commute, no argument there. But after four days of panels, side meetings and client dinners, many of our guests tell us the thing they value most is what happens after work: quiet. No sirens at 2 am, no thin walls, no queue for the lift.

From Table View, the drive to the CTICC, the V&A Waterfront or the city centre takes around 25 minutes. At rush hour, allow a little extra time, and perhaps a decent podcast. The airport is about 20 minutes away, which makes that early flight home far less painful.

## Our insider tip

Coming for Mining Indaba? Fly in on the Friday. The weekend before the conference, the **Cape Grand Prix Revival** takes over Killarney International Raceway, about 5 minutes from the lodge, from 5 to 7 February 2027, with historic Grand Prix cars on track. A far more enjoyable warm-up for the week than another round of emails.

And on conference evenings: close the laptop, walk five minutes to the beach and watch the light change on Table Mountain. It's the best reset we know before day two.
