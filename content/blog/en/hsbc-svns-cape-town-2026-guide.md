Picture this: a stadium full of superheroes, a few hundred referees who've never refereed anything, and at least one person dressed as a slice of watermelon – all on their feet as the Blitzboks break down the wing. That's the **HSBC SVNS Cape Town**, back at the **DHL Stadium** in Green Point on **5 and 6 December 2026**. Locals simply call it the best party of the year, and it's hard to argue.

## Sevens in a nutshell

Sevens is rugby on fast-forward: seven players a side instead of fifteen, two short halves and acres of space to run into. Matches are over almost before you've found your seat, and the next one kicks off moments later – so a single day gives you a lot of rugby for your money.

The **world's best men's and women's sevens teams** come to Cape Town, and the home side has a big mission. According to SA Rugby, the **Blitzboks** are the reigning world champions and are chasing a **third successive world title** – this time with a home crowd behind them. Expect the noise levels to match.

## Tickets and planning

- **Tickets** are sold through Ticketmaster, linked from SA Rugby's official event page. It's a popular weekend, so don't leave it to the last minute.
- **Match schedule and kick-off times** are published by the organisers ahead of the tournament – check the official page before you plan your day around a particular game.
- **Missed out on tickets?** Every match is live on SuperSport, and plenty of spots around town will have it on.
- **Fancy dress is half the fun.** Cape Town's Sevens crowd is famous for its costumes. There's no dress code – just our one piece of advice: choose an outfit you'll still be happy sitting in by six o'clock.

## Getting there

The DHL Stadium sits in Green Point, between the city centre and the V&A Waterfront – around **25 minutes by car** from us. Match days are busy around the stadium, so we'd suggest leaving the car at the lodge and taking an **Uber** both ways. If the City runs shuttles or special traffic arrangements, they're usually announced on the City of Cape Town's channels shortly before the event.

Early December is proper summer here: long light evenings, plenty of sun and, quite often, a stiff breeze. Sunscreen is non-negotiable.

## Our local tip

Make a whole weekend of it. After day one, Uber back, grab a bite in the garden and get an early night (we know – but trust us). On Sunday morning, start with a walk on the beach – just a **5-minute stroll** from the lodge, Table Mountain view included – then head back for day two.

Whether anything happens on the water is entirely up to the wind, so we can't promise a thing. But if the south-easter is pumping, swing by Kite Beach in the late afternoon – a good 10-minute walk from us. Rugby on Saturday, kites on Sunday: not a bad way to spend a weekend.
