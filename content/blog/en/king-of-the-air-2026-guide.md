When the south-easter comes howling over Table Mountain, Bloubergstrand becomes the stage for the world's most extreme kite contest: **Red Bull King of the Air**. From **21 November to 6 December 2026**, the best Big Air riders on the planet go head to head – with jumps that'll have you looking skywards all afternoon.

Our advice: pack the sunscreen and warm up your neck.

## When's the action? The wind decides.

There's no fixed schedule. The 16 days are a **waiting window**: organisers watch wind strength, direction and swell, and call competition days at short notice. Day one on the calendar isn't necessarily day one on the water. Around here, the wind is the boss.

**Our tip:** keep your plans flexible and, if you can, stay a few extra days. Check the official Red Bull event page for the call.

## Where to watch

The finals launch from **Kite Beach, Bloubergstrand**, and watching is **free** – from the sand or the beachfront promenade. The closer to the competition zone, the better the view.

- **Get there early** – on the big days the beach fills up fast.
- **Dress for the wind** – windbreaker, sunglasses, sunscreen. Leave the hat at home, or it might get more airtime than the riders.
- **Take a break** – cafés and restaurants along the beachfront offer a welcome escape from the Cape Doctor.

## What is Big Air, anyway?

Big Air is all about maximum height, long hang time and spectacular tricks – kiteloops, rotations, board-offs – judged on height, difficulty and style. With strong, reliable wind and Table Mountain as the backdrop, Bloubergstrand is one of the best arenas in the world for it. We might be just a little biased.

## Our local tip

Even on non-competition days, the beach is worth it: many pro riders train here in the weeks around the event. Late afternoon is best – golden light, kites against the sun and dogs chasing the waves.
