Cape Town is one of the best-known kitesurfing destinations in the world, and Bloubergstrand is at the heart of it. Every summer, kiters from Europe and beyond come for the reliable wind, the waves and that unbeatable view of Table Mountain while riding.

## The wind: meet the Cape Doctor

The main kite season runs roughly from November to March. During these months the south-easterly wind, nicknamed the "Cape Doctor", blows strongly on many afternoons. It usually picks up after lunch and is often at its best in the late afternoon. Mornings are frequently calmer, which leaves time for the beach, a hike or a lazy breakfast.

## The spots near our lodge

**Kite Beach** in Bloubergstrand, about 10 minutes from us, is the classic: waves, a cross-shore wind and the mountain behind you. It is best suited to riders who are already confident. **Big Bay**, a little further north, is popular for wave riding and surfing. Beginners often head up the West Coast to the flat, shallow **Langebaan Lagoon**, about an hour's drive along the R27, which is ideal for first lessons.

## What beginners should know

The Atlantic here is cold, even in summer, so a good wetsuit (a 4/3 mm or thicker) is a must. The wind can be strong and gusty, so take lessons with a certified local school rather than going out alone. Many schools rent out complete gear, so you do not need to bring your own.
