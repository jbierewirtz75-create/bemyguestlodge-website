Summer on this stretch of coast has a rhythm. Mornings belong to the runners and the dog walkers. By lunchtime the south-easter has usually turned up uninvited, and the kites follow it out onto the water. And in the evening, half of Table View seems to wander down to the sand to watch the sun drop behind the Atlantic, with Table Mountain glowing across the bay.

We've been watching that show for a while now, and we still haven't tired of it. Here's our pick of what's on between now and the end of summer – most of it within easy reach of our garden.

## Your beach, five minutes away

From the lodge it's a **5-minute walk** to the beach. No car, no parking, just flip-flops and a towel.

The neighbourhood beachfront is getting a proper glow-up, too. The City has been upgrading the Table View coastline, and a new coastal walkway of around two kilometres is already open. The final phase, mainly new ablution facilities, is due to be finished **by mid-December**, just in time for the festive season – provided the work stays on schedule.

Fancy a swim at an award-winning beach? **Melkbosstrand**, roughly 15 minutes' drive up the coast, holds Blue Flag status for the 2026/27 season. A word of warning about the water on this side of the peninsula: it's bracing. Locals call it refreshing. Visitors tend to use other words.

## Kite season is here

That same wind makes Bloubergstrand one of the world's great kite spots. The **Red Bull King of the Air** waiting window runs until **6 December 2026** at Kite Beach, a good 10-minute walk from us, and watching is free. When it actually happens is up to the wind – our [King of the Air spectator guide](/en/blog/king-of-the-air-2026-guide/) has everything you need.

## Sunday sunsets at Kirstenbosch

Few things say Cape Town summer like the **Kirstenbosch Summer Sunset Concerts**, held in the botanical garden on the slopes of Table Mountain, around 35 minutes by car. The season runs **every Sunday until 28 March 2027**. Booshle G and Freshlyground play on 29 November, and Matthew Mole and Congo Cowboys see in the new year on **New Year's Eve**. There's also a special concert by **Leon Bridges** on Monday 30 November.

## The festive season, Cape style

On **Sunday 29 November**, the city switches on its **Festive Lights** with a free street party from 4pm to 10pm on the Grand Parade and Adderley Street, with a big line-up of local acts. It's busy, it's loud and it's great fun – just leave the car at home.

Missing a proper Christmas market? On **Saturdays from 28 November to 19 December**, 9am to 3pm, a German-inspired market pops up in Paarden Eiland, around 15 minutes from us – free entry. Mulled-wine nostalgia in 28 degrees: only in Cape Town.

## Also on, practically next door

- **Sunday 29 November:** the Cape Town Toy Run at Killarney Raceway, about 5 minutes away – entry is a donated toy.
- **Saturday 12 December:** **Get Lucky Summer** at Table View Primary, about 5 minutes away, with GoodLuck and Prime Circle among the acts. Possibly the shortest walk home from a festival you'll ever have. The same day, Killarney hosts the WPMC 8 Hour endurance race.
- **15 to 17 January 2027:** **Jazzathon** at the Amphitheatre, V&A Waterfront, around 25 minutes by car – free entry.

For everything else that's on near the lodge, see our always-current [events calendar](/en/events/).

## Our local tip

For Kirstenbosch, head out early and pack a picnic – a blanket, snacks and something to drink – because the best spots on the lawn go quickly. And on the evenings with nothing planned? Skip the crowds in town and walk down to the beach for sunset. It's free, it's five minutes away, and no two are ever the same.
