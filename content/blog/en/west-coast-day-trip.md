Most visitors head south from Cape Town, to the Cape Peninsula and Cape Point. But just north of us lies a quieter side of the Western Cape: the West Coast, with wide beaches, fishing villages, fresh seafood and, in spring, carpets of wildflowers. The best part: the R27 coastal road starts right on our doorstep.

## Langebaan and the lagoon

About an hour's drive north, the town of Langebaan sits on a huge, turquoise lagoon. The shallow, calm water is perfect for families, swimming and learning to kitesurf, and it is home to many water birds, including flamingos. Have lunch at one of the relaxed restaurants overlooking the water.

## West Coast National Park

The West Coast National Park surrounds the lagoon and is ideal for a scenic drive with plenty of stops. Look out for antelope, ostriches and tortoises. In late winter and spring, usually around August and September, the park's Postberg section bursts into colour with wildflowers, one of the most beautiful sights in South Africa.

## Paternoster

If you have a little more time, continue to Paternoster, a picturesque fishing village with white cottages, granite rocks and some excellent seafood restaurants. It is around an hour and a half from Blouberg.

## Our tip

Leave after breakfast, take the coast road and be back in time for sunset over Table Mountain on Blouberg beach. We are happy to help you plan the route and suggest our favourite places for lunch.
