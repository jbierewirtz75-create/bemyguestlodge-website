## 2. Visiting this website

**Server data.** When you open our website, the server of our hosting provider ({{TODO: name of the hosting provider}}) automatically processes technical data that your browser sends: IP address, date and time, the page requested, the referring page, browser and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website and to keep it secure. The data is stored only for a short time and is not combined with other data.

**No cookies.** Our website does not use cookies. Your browser only remembers your chosen language and whether you closed a notice (in so-called local storage on your own device); this information is not sent to us.

**Fonts.** The fonts are loaded from our own server; no connection to third-party font services is made.

**Visitor statistics (Umami).** To understand how our website is used, we use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service. Umami works without cookies, does not store full IP addresses and does not create personal profiles. We only see aggregated figures, such as page views, the referring website or campaign (UTM parameters), device type, browser, country and clicks on buttons such as "Book direct" or WhatsApp. The data is processed by Umami (umami.is) on servers in the European Union (EU). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving our website (POPIA section 11(1)(f); GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

**"How did you hear about us?"** If you answer this optional question, only your answer (for example "Instagram") is recorded as an anonymous statistic, without any link to your person.

## 3. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, WhatsApp or phone, we process your name, contact details and the content of your message to answer your enquiry and, if applicable, to prepare your booking. WhatsApp is a service of WhatsApp/Meta; when you use it, their privacy policy also applies. If you prefer, simply write to us by email.

## 4. Bookings and your stay

**Booking system.** Online bookings are processed through NightsBridge. Your booking details (such as name, contact details, travel dates, number of guests and payment details) are entered there and processed by NightsBridge on our behalf; card details are handled by the payment provider, not by us. For bookings through other platforms (for example Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, Agoda or LekkeSlaap), the platform passes your booking details to us; their own privacy policies apply as well.

**Your stay.** For your stay we process the information needed to accommodate you, for example names of the guests, contact details, arrival and departure, special requests and invoices. We keep booking and accounting records for as long as required by law (in South Africa generally five years for tax purposes) and then delete them.

Legal basis: performing your booking (POPIA section 11(1)(b); GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligations (POPIA section 11(1)(c); GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

## 5. Sharing and transfers

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who support us (operators under POPIA, for example hosting, booking system, email and analytics) and only to the extent necessary, or with authorities if we are legally required to do so. Some of these providers are located outside South Africa. In that case we make sure that your information is adequately protected, as required by POPIA section 72 and, where applicable, the GDPR.

## 6. Links to other websites

Our website links to other services, for example Google Maps, WhatsApp, booking platforms and social media. Information is only transferred to these services when you click the link; from then on, their privacy policies apply.

## 7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for this website.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us at any time which information we hold about you, and you can request that it be corrected or deleted. You can object to the processing of your information, and you can withdraw a consent you have given at any time with effect for the future. Under the GDPR, you also have the right to restriction of processing and to data portability. Simply write to [info@bemyguestlodge.co.za](mailto:info@bemyguestlodge.co.za).

If you believe that we are not handling your information correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the **Information Regulator (South Africa)**, website [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za), email POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za. Residents of the European Union can also contact the data protection authority in their country.

## 9. Changes

We update this policy when our website or the law changes. The current version always applies.
