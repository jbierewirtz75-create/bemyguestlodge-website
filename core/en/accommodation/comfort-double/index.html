Room 2

Comfort Double Room

Crisp navy and white set the tone in Room 2, finished with blue Delft-style tiles at the basin and slate-grey curtains. The double bed with its navy throw invites you to sink in after a day at the beach, while the ceiling fan, writing desk and door to the garden make the room as practical as it is cosy.

Room 3

Comfort Double Room

Room 3 is light and serene: soft white walls, turquoise and gold ginkgo prints and a leaf-patterned rug give it a gentle botanical feel. Hand-painted turquoise and yellow tiles at the basin add a cheerful touch, and the quiet outlook to the garden makes it an easy place to unwind.

Room 4

Comfort Double Room

Lush greens define Room 4, our studio: tropical palm wallpaper, a leafy rug, an emerald-tiled bathroom and a pink flamingo print set a relaxed holiday mood. With its own kitchenette, dining nook and extra space, it is made for longer stays and guests who like to cook a little.
