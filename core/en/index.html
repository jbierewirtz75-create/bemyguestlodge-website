Welcome to Bloubergstrand

Let's make Be My Guest your home in Cape Town.

Be My Guest Lodge is a family-run guesthouse in Bloubergrant, Table View, part of the popular seaside area of Bloubergstrand on Cape Town's Atlantic coast. Robben Island lies just across the bay, the beach is minutes away, and Cape Town International Airport is about a 20-minute drive.

Your hosts Jan & Leona welcome up to 16 guests in five comfort rooms and a spacious family cottage, on a B&B or self-catering basis. From here, Cape Town's highlights are easy to reach: the V&A Waterfront, Table Mountain and the Cape Peninsula. For a day trip up the West Coast to Langebaan or Lambert's Bay, the R27 coastal road starts right on our doorstep.

Whether you're planning a family holiday, a kitesurfing trip or a business stay, Bloubergstrand is the place to be. As the afternoon wind picks up, the beachfront becomes a playground for kitesurfers, joggers and beachcombers. And there's no better view of Table Mountain than from Blouberg beach: at sunset the mountain glows in shades of blue and violet, which gave the area its name, “Blouberg”, Afrikaans for “blue mountain”. It's the time of day when families gather on the beach, and when you get hungry, plenty of restaurants line the beachfront.

Our Accommodation

Three room types, six places to unwind.

Be My Guest Lodge sleeps up to 16 guests on a B&B or self-catering basis: three Comfort Double Rooms, two Comfort Twin Rooms and our spacious two-storey Family Cottage. Every room has its own bathroom, a private entrance and a patio opening onto the garden.

In each room you'll find air conditioning that cools in summer and heats in winter, a ceiling fan, a flat-screen TV with DStv, free Wi-Fi, a mini fridge, a microwave and tea and coffee making facilities. The Family Cottage adds its own fully equipped kitchen, two bathrooms and a large terrace by the pool.

Outside, the lush garden and swimming pool are yours to enjoy. Two braais (barbecues) are available to all guests, perfect for a relaxed South African evening by the pool with family and friends. See rates and availability directly in our booking system.

FAQ

Good to know before you arrive.

Where is Be My Guest Lodge located?

Be My Guest Lodge is at 51 Porterfield Rd in Bloubergrant, Table View, part of Bloubergstrand on Cape Town's Atlantic coast. The beach is about a 5-minute walk away, Kite Beach a good 10-minute walk (3–4 minutes by car), and the V&A Waterfront about 25 minutes by car.

How far is the lodge from Cape Town International Airport?

The drive from Cape Town International Airport takes about 20 minutes. On request, we are happy to organise a taxi for you.

Is Be My Guest Lodge suitable for families?

Yes. Our Family Cottage sleeps up to six guests on two floors, with its own kitchen, two bathrooms and a terrace by the pool. Families also enjoy the garden, the swimming pool and the two braais (barbecues), and the beach is just minutes away. A baby cot and a high chair are available on request.

Is the lodge a good base for kitesurfing in Cape Town?

Yes. Kite Beach in Bloubergstrand is a good 10-minute walk or 3–4 minutes by car, and the main kite season runs roughly from November to March. Every room has a private entrance and patio where you can rinse and dry your gear, and we are happy to help you find lessons or rental equipment.

Can we stay on a B&B or self-catering basis?

Both are possible. All rooms have a mini fridge, microwave and tea and coffee facilities, Room 4 has a kitchenette, and the Family Cottage has a fully equipped kitchen.

Do the rooms have air conditioning?

Yes. Every room has air conditioning that cools in summer and heats in winter, plus a ceiling fan, free Wi-Fi and a flat-screen TV with DStv.

Is there secure parking?

Yes. Parking is right on the property, in a quiet and safe residential neighbourhood.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is between 2 pm and 7 pm, and check-out is until 10 am. Late check-in is possible, just let us know in advance via WhatsApp or email.

Can we book the whole lodge for a group?

Yes. The whole lodge, with five rooms and the Family Cottage, can be booked exclusively for groups of up to 16 guests, ideal for family celebrations, kite crews or team trips. Message us on WhatsApp or email for an offer.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are welcome in the Family Cottage. If you would like to bring more than one pet, please ask us first.

Do you offer special rates for longer stays?

Yes. For longer stays, ask us for a tailored rate. Just tell us your dates via WhatsApp or email.

How can we book directly?

You can book directly in our online booking system, which shows available rooms in real time. For questions, message us on WhatsApp at +27 72 838 4490 or email info@bemyguestlodge.co.za.
